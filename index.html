The Manifesto · Folio 0

This archive maps the oldest temptation in science: the claim that occult and metaphysical concepts correspond to physical reality. Tesla’s resonant earth, Newton’s living mercury, Jung and Pauli’s one world, the Schumann cavity humming beneath every brain — we take each claim seriously enough to check it.

The finding that organizes everything here is an equivocation: most occult–physics correspondences fail not because the physics is absent, but because a measurable quantity — frequency, field, resonance, vacuum energy — is silently swapped for an undefined homonym carrying no units, no instrument, and no distinguishing prediction.

So we grade every correspondence. A human has spectra, not a frequency — and every claim in this archive states its evidence, its coherence timescale, its substrate, and its falsifier, or it wears the badge that says it cannot.

The Six Grades

Established  Documented history or textbook physics with replicated measurements.

Frontier  Real, published, methodologically serious; the central claim unreplicated or contested. Every Frontier grade states in advance what would move it up or down.

Speculative  A genuine conceptual analogy or philosophical position — not an empirical claim.

Untestable  No instrument, unit, or observation could count against it. Not disproven — a different, and worse, epistemic status.

Refuted  Made a testable commitment and lost: failed replication, decisive critique, or forbidden by established physics.

Misattributed  A fabricated quote, a false genealogy, or a category error — a real phenomenon named for something it is not.

The grading-object rule, binding on every row of the Atlas: the badge grades the correspondence as popularly asserted — not the occult concept alone, and not the physics alone.

Open Frontier

Real research · central claim unproven
  • Schumann–EEG/HRV correlations — small-sample, correlational, mechanism-free at ~1 pT; awaiting a bound-setting null (Experiment B1).
  • Human magnetoreception — one controlled positive EEG study (Wang 2019), unreplicated; avian radical-pair chemistry the strongest quantum-biology candidate.
  • Orch OR — falsifiable, partially constrained: Tegmark’s decoherence stands; Donadi 2021 ruled out the simplest model; microtubule optics keep the substrate interesting.
  • Connectome-harmonic signatures of consciousness level — established as method, maturing as biomarker.
  • Dual-aspect monism and General Resonance Theory — legitimate philosophy with physics vocabulary; untested.
  • Ultraweak photon emission as a diagnostic optical marker of brain activity.
  • AARO-era UAP assessment — open as airspace safety; closed, negative, as extraterrestrial technology per the official record.

Refuted & Apocryphal

Plus the untestable — a worse status, not a synonym
  • The Tesla quote canon: “energy, frequency and vibration,” “3, 6, 9,” “Earth is a realm” — apocryphal, internet-era, or fabricated.
  • Tesla’s withheld breakthroughs: John G. Trump’s 1943 report found nothing workable; the papers went to Belgrade in 1952.
  • Scalar waves; macroscopic torsion fields; Kozyrev’s unreplicated causal-mechanics experiments.
  • Free-energy and information-storage readings of the quantum vacuum.
  • Tainio’s MHz body scale; Hawkins’ kinesiology numbers; Rife frequencies; Solfeggio “528 Hz DNA repair.”
  • “Quantum mechanics proves consciousness creates reality” — decoherence is observer-free.
  • Global Consciousness Project — chance-level on reanalysis. HAARP weather/mind-control claims — physically impossible. “CIA proved astral projection” — a desk study, misread.
  • Untestable  Synchronicity-as-physics, the astral plane, the Akashic field, morphic resonance, Kozyrev “time energy”: no distinguishing prediction exists.
