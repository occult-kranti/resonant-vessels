Act I

The Argument

The Advisor

The equivocation diagnosis is the spine of this project: most occult–physics correspondences fail not because the physics is absent but because a measurable quantity is silently swapped for an undefined homonym carrying no units, no instrument, and no distinguishing prediction. My Round 1 job was to find the sentence that would get us laughed out of a faculty seminar; I found six. Here is what happened to them.

On grading — Refuted is not the wastebasket
Advisor

Your table graded synchronicity, the astral plane, the Akashic record, and Kozyrev’s “time energy” as Refuted — while your own essays describe them as making no distinguishing prediction. You cannot experimentally refute what predicts nothing.

Research Assistant

Conceded — the most consequential fix of the round. The scheme now has six grades: Untestable — no instrument, unit, or observation could count against the claim — sits beside Refuted, and five rows moved into it. Each verdict still records which testable sub-claims failed: Kozyrev’s experiments committed and failed replication.

Advisor

Calling unfalsifiable metaphysics “disproven” hands the believer a rebuttal for free. The ledger’s new gloss should be read aloud at every editorial meeting: unfalsifiable is not a synonym for disproven; it is a different and worse epistemic status. Refuted means you played and lost. Untestable means you never showed up.

The amplitude reality-check — 1 pT against 50 µT
Advisor

Now the flagship. Row 6 — Schumann–EEG coupling — was graded Frontier without facing the discipline Essay E demands of everyone else. State the amplitudes.

Research Assistant

The Schumann magnetic component is ~1 pT. The one positive controlled human magnetoreception study, Wang et al. 2019, used fields at Earth strength — ~50 µT, seven orders of magnitude stronger — and remains unreplicated. Of the candidate transducers: radical-pair chemistry needs µT-class fields and lacks in vivo proof even in birds; magnetite torque at pT ELF is unknown in humans; ephaptic coupling is endogenous, not a pT receiver.

Advisor

So no candidate transducer operates at picotesla ELF against thermal noise. The coupling is not merely unproven; it is mechanism-free at the relevant amplitude. Yet B1 carried a ~5% prior. Five percent for a coupling with no candidate transducer is not a prior — it is politeness.

Research Assistant

Conceded on mechanism, defended on design. B1 is redesignated in writing as a bound-setting null measurement: the deliverable is the tightest shielded-room upper bound on SR–EEG coupling yet published, and the 5% survives only as a labeled, subjective unknown-unknown allowance. The shielding fix matters too: µ-metal attenuation degrades steeply below 10 Hz — exactly at Schumann frequencies — so it must now be measured at 7.83 and 14.1 Hz, with an active-cancellation fallback.

Advisor

Right answer. B1 now carries the label plus a falsifiable-in-advance clause: upgrade on preregistered, shielding-abolished, replicated coherence; downgrade to Refuted at tested amplitudes on a clean null. That is what a Frontier badge should mean.

A7 — apples and teslas
Advisor

The rebuttal of the Bentov/Gateway 7 Hz claim committed the very category error this site exists to prosecute — it answered a mechanical body-on-support resonance by citing magnetic field amplitudes. A careful reader sees us comparing apples to teslas.

Research Assistant

Rewritten, not deleted. A7 now keeps two channels strictly separate: the mechanical 4–8 Hz ballistocardiographic resonance is genuine biomechanics; the field amplitudes are electromagnetism. The Gateway claim asserts the first resonates with the second, and the verdict is now the simpler, stronger one you demanded: no coupling chain — mechanical oscillation to electromagnetic transduction to a field at distance — exists or has been proposed at any relevant amplitude. Full stop.

The 0.08484 seconds
Research Assistant

Here I push back — partially. You flagged A6’s “antipodal round trip ≥ 0.08484 s at 471,240 km/s” as inconsistent, offering ~0.054 s. But the full text of US 787,412 has the wave “passing to and returning from the region diametrically opposite the pole over the earth’s surface” — over the surface, out and back is one full 40,000 km transit, and 40,000 ÷ 471,240 ≈ 0.0849 s. Your 0.054 s assumes a through-Earth chord the patent disclaims.

Advisor

On that point I concede — my alternative presumed a geometry Tesla refused. But the original sentence was still the kind of slip that gets screenshotted, because a reader could not tell which geometry produced it. The corrected text states the geometry, the verbatim language, and that 471,240 km/s ≈ (π/2)·c — a surface phase velocity, not a signal speed. Ambiguity, not error, was the exposure. The rest of the patent census came back clean.

B8 — the most expensive proposal resting on its weakest statistic
Advisor

Success was “Spearman ρ > 0.8 across ≥4 agents.” At n = 4, even a perfect rank correlation gives one-tailed p ≈ 0.042 — 1/4!. The statistic could not carry the inferential weight assigned to it.

Research Assistant

Correct arithmetic; the endpoint has been rebuilt. The series is extended to n ≥ 8 named agents plus ≥4 inactive controls — at n = 8, ρ = 0.8 gives p ≈ 0.017; at n = 10, p < 0.01. If the series cannot be extended, the MAC endpoint is explicitly downgraded to descriptive, inference reverting to the fully powered agent-versus-control contrast. The fallback is written into the protocol, not left as a promise.

Do connectome “harmonics” deserve the resonance label?
Advisor

My last structural worry was self-directed. Essay A proclaims resonance “the one bridge that truly holds” — while approvingly citing connectome harmonics, which are Laplacian eigenmodes of a graph. No oscillator, no drive, no damping. Is Atasoy’s usage metaphor by our own criterion?

Research Assistant

The licensing rule, in one sentence: resonance and harmonics language is licensed wherever there is a linear eigenmode decomposition of a measurable dynamical system — a specified operator, measurable modes, and state-dependent projections onto them, the driven damped oscillator at measured Q being the strongest case — and forbidden wherever no system, operator, modes, or measurement is specified. A driven oscillator is the sufficient case, not the necessary one. Atasoy passes; “your frequency is low” fails every clause.

Advisor

The sentence that earns the triumphalism is the concession: the metaphor boundary runs through mainstream neuroscience, not around it. B3’s own result — eigenfrequencies belong to the object-plus-boundary system — makes the same point from the physics side. The flagship survives scrutiny because it consented to be scrutinized.

Act II

Final Adjudication — the six unresolved items

Item 1 — Row 28’s split-verdict convention · Verdict: adopt dual grading with a precedence rule

Kozyrev’s program is a mixed claim — a testable skirt around an unfalsifiable core — and rows 22, 24, and 25 share the structure. The convention: the badge grades the core claim as popularly asserted; any testable sub-component that committed and failed is recorded in the verdict as “testable component: refuted,” never silently absorbed into the badge. Untestable takes precedence because it names the claim’s terminal epistemic status — after the testable parts are stripped away and fail, what remains predicts nothing. The rule now stands as a sentence in the grading scheme itself.

Item 2 — B1’s 5% prior · Verdict: keep the labeled nonzero prior; the rename is rejected, with a tightening

The Assistant offered a fair fork — a small prior as the price of calling B1 a hypothesis test, or a rename to “measurement”; the Advisor takes the first tine. B1 retains genuine hypothesis-test structure, and a stated prior of exactly zero would be false precision in the opposite direction: unknown-unknown transduction is not logically excluded, only mechanism-free at present. In exchange: the 5% is labeled a subjective allowance, not a mechanism-based estimate, and the justification for funding B1 is the bound, not the positive. That is no longer politeness; it is honesty with a hedge attached.

Item 3 — Row 29’s grade floor · Verdict: Frontier stands

Frontier means real, published, methodologically serious research whose central claim remains unreplicated or contested, and the sigil correspondence satisfies that definition in its strongest honest reading: the invoked constructs are real psychological objects, meta-analyzed for implementation intentions at d ≈ 0.65 (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) — a sturdier base rate than the subliminal-priming family — and the chaos-magick literature itself often adopts the psychological reading. Speculative would undersell documented constructs; Established would oversell untested practice. One condition: Row 29 now carries the same falsifiable-in-advance clause as rows 6 and 38 — if implementation-intention effect sizes collapse, or an RCT of sigil practice returns null, the row downgrades. The constructs must remain hostage to their evidence.

Item 4 — Rows 14/16 grading · Verdict: correspondence-grading upheld; both rows stay Speculative

The binding grading-object rule — the badge evaluates the correspondence as popularly asserted, not the concept alone and not the analog alone — is the right rule for a site whose object of study is correspondences. Concept-grading would duplicate the verdict column, which already states concept-status (“refuted as chymistry”) and analog-status (“Established nuclear physics”) separately.

Item 5 — Row 20’s boundary · Verdict: unus mundus stays Speculative

The Speculative/Untestable boundary is not “how wrong is it” but “how is it offered.” Jung defined the unus mundus as “a postulate” (CW 14 ¶767); Atmanspacher–Primas formalize it as philosophy, labeled philosophy, with no physics costume. Untestable is the grade for metaphysics dressed as physics; Speculative is for a philosophical position that knows what it is. The scheme rewards self-aware restraint, since the alternative creates a perverse incentive: dress your metaphysics in physics and you get the same badge as honest philosophy.

Item 6 — The 0.08484 s patent-faithfulness · Verdict: the fix stands; a general convention is adopted

The original wording was patent-faithful, and the Round-1 alternative of ~0.054 s presumed a through-Earth chord the patent disclaims — the geometry is conceded. But patent-faithfulness is necessary, not sufficient, for a site whose brand is numerical exactitude: a figure a reader cannot reconstruct is an exposure whether or not it is faithful. Convention adopted: every historical number quoted on the site must encode its own derivation. This exchange, publicly logged, is the brand working as intended.

What this project demonstrates

The occult–physics comparison is worth doing rigorously precisely because the lazy versions fail in both directions. The believer’s version — Tesla knew, the CIA proved, quantum mechanics says so — collapses on contact with primary sources: the quote traces to a purple-plate vendor, the Gateway report is a desk study, the founders of quantum theory explicitly denied the popular use. But the debunker’s version collapses too, and more quietly, because it never expects to be checked. It calls unfalsifiable metaphysics “disproven.” It answers a mechanical resonance with a magnetic amplitude. It grades Newton’s alchemy as simple error and misses the documented, replicable bench chemistry under the Decknamen. Disciplined comparison is harder than either, and it is the only version that survives being quoted.

What the discipline yields, unexpectedly, is content rather than absence. Newton’s failed sophic-mercury campaign is a falsified hypothesis preserved in a notebook — a documented null result from 1670, scientific content of the first rank. Tesla’s wrong attribution produced a real observation and, fifty years later, a vindicated intuition in a corrected medium. Jung and Pauli’s metaphysics, graded honestly, turns out to be serious philosophy with clinically real phenomenology. The residue of rigorous debunking is not a vacuum; it is a map of where the genuine bridges actually are — resonance, spectroscopy, biomagnetism at femtotesla scale — and every one of them is quantitative.

The cargo-cult warning runs at full strength in both directions, and Feynman’s formulation is the project’s north star: the first principle is that you must not fool yourself. The occult marketplace fools itself with borrowed vocabulary — spectra without instruments, frequencies without units. But a skeptical site fools itself the moment it stops checking its own arithmetic, its own priors, its own geometry of antipodal round trips. This roundtable is published in full precisely so that the corrections are part of the product. A resource about the boundary between sense and nonsense has to live on that boundary, in public, dated and logged.

— The Advisor, final round. The roundtable is closed; the ledger remains open, versioned, and public.
