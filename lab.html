The Panel Record · Rounds I–V

What each round settled

Round 1 — The Advisor’s Review

The strongest entries stood: S2 (the ERP-superposition forward model, “load-bearing — no EEG dollar until it exists”), S1 (the power audit), A3 (the Chladni demonstration). The collapse was structural: S1’s pass criterion demanded Type-I ≤ 5.5% and ≥ 80% power at d = 0.35 with ten sessions — arithmetically impossible, “a gate the pipeline is guaranteed to fail.” Blinding by sealed software key was judged one DevTools click from unblinding; guess-the-arm checks existed nowhere; and the portfolio ran ~13 hypotheses with no familywise or FDR policy above the single-test level.

Round 2 — The Harness Runs

The Researcher conceded with computed numbers: at minSessions = 10, power at d = 0.35 is 6.8%; sixty sessions reaches only ~23%; 80% power needs ≈128 sessions/arm at d = 0.35 (≈63/arm at d = 0.5). The Advisor’s independent reproduction — ideal-Gaussian Welch power ~11% at n = 10 — bracketed the harness figure from above, the gap attributable to Likert-7 discretization and carryover attenuation. The remediation tree was preregistered: R0 run the grid, R1 exact permutation (“validity repair, not power repair”), R2 raise minSessions, R3 widen the SESOI or demote. Every historical Quick Lab “signal” verdict migrates to “unaudited” until recomputed. The Gate

Statistics first, stimuli second— no human-subject entry (A1, A2, A4, A5, A6, B3, B4) proceeds past stimulus construction until the S1 gate resolves to a passing branch. Portfolio law G1/G5.

The S1 statistics audit, for lay readers

Every human experiment in this laboratory would run on one instrument: the Quick Lab engine inside the open-sync repository — the code that decides, after two blinded weeks of self-experiment, whether the tonic did anything. Before the lab trusts that instrument with a single subject, S1 feeds it synthetic data with known truth and counts how often it cries “signal” on pure noise (a false-positive, or Type-I, rate) and how often it misses a real, moderate effect (power). Twenty thousand replications per cell; three hundred sixty cells; the real engine code path, checked against a bit-exact port through a live bridge.

The verdict has two halves. Half one: the instrument is fine when sessions interleave. Under the crossover mapping — arm A one day, arm B the next — the false-positive rate sits at ≈ .05 at every autocorrelation tested, exactly where a 5% test should sit. Half two: the instrument lies when sessions bunch into blocks. Real self-experimenters do not interleave; they do two weeks of A, then two weeks of B. When consecutive days are correlated (ρ = 0.95 — Monday predicts Tuesday), the blocked mapping inflates the false-positive rate to 0.608 ± 0.003: on pure noise, the engine reports “signal” three times out of five. That reproduces the Advisor’s independent anchor (.62 ± .03) and adjudicates the repo’s own critique R2 — whose 65.6% is excluded at the acceptance window’s edge, printed, and argued with in the open rather than quietly re-aimed at. This is the blocked-pseudoreplication failure: correlated days are not independent coin flips, and counting them as if they were manufactures certainty out of weather.

And the power half, the finding that organized Round 2: detecting a moderate effect (d = 0.35) at the repo’s default enrollment of ten sessions per arm succeeds 6.8% of the time — a gate the pipeline was guaranteed to fail. Reaching 80% power at that effect size needs ≈128 sessions per arm; at a larger effect (d = 0.5), ≈64. The gate is therefore not a formality: it is the arithmetic that decides what the lab is allowed to ask of a human being.

The preregistered remediation tree

  1. R0 — run: the harness grid above. Pass = Type-I ≤ 5.5% and ≥ 80% power at the entry’s SESOI. (Failed by construction at minSessions = 10; that failure is the finding.)
  2. R1 — permutation repair: swap the Welch t for an exact permutation test over the enrolled-seed schedule. SIM-2 below shows the repair is real but bounded: it restores validity (Type-I ≤ .055 at ρ = 0.95) only when enough blocks exist to permute — ten or more block-pairs. Below that, the test cannot even express p ≤ .05 (two-sided, K pairs allow minimum p = 2/2K) and controls the error rate at zero by discreteness — vacuous, printed, and never counted as powerful. Validity repair, not power repair.
  3. R2 — raise the n: minSessions moves to the smallest grid value reaching 80% power: ≈128 sessions/arm at d = 0.35, ≈63/arm at d = 0.5. Every historical Quick Lab “signal” verdict is re-labeled “unaudited” until recomputed under the corrected test and n.
  4. R3 — widen or demote: where R2’s n is infeasible, the entry’s SESOI is widened pre-data to what feasible n supports, or the entry is demoted to descriptive/equivalence-only.

Governance references: the S1 harness and the R0–R3 tree are specified in the panel documents (portfolio §0/G1; sim_design §SIM-1/2); the docs tree is listed under Docs & Reproducibility.

S2

The ERP-Superposition Forward Model

Infrastructure $0 compute
Convincing power: yes — internal + EEG-methods readers; load-bearing: no EEG dollar until it exists

The question that decides whether “40 Hz entrainment” claims mean anything: can a superposition of ordinary stimulus-locked responses — no oscillator changed, nothing entrained — masquerade as steady-state entrainment? S2 builds both worlds synthetically: H_super, EEG as a sum of damped-cosine offset-ERP kernels from a preregistered family (base × {refractory, adaptation, neither} × morphology ±20%, frozen at commit before any sweep), and H_ent, a true oscillator. Then it asks what discriminates them.

The amplitude-matching disclosure, stated in the instrument’s own metrics: the superposition family is amplitude-matched to the entrainment world, and its family-max ON-window 40 Hz peak reproduces H_ent’s peak within 6.54% — inside the 10% acceptance band. Amplitude cannot tell the two worlds apart. Only OFF-window persistence (signal surviving ≥ 1 s after stimulus offset) discriminates, and it does so decisively: escape rate 1.0 for τ_off ∈ {1, 1.5, 2} s at SNR 1.0, AUC 0.933 at SNR ≥ 0.2, false escapes on H_super at 1.1% against a ≈1% target calibrated on ≥ 1,000 surrogates. Below SNR 0.1 the instrument says so: indistinguishable.
