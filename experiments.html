The Correspondence Atlas

The grading scheme

Six grades. Established documented history or textbook physics with replicated measurements. Frontier real, published, methodologically serious research whose central claim remains unreplicated or contested — every such row states in advance what observation would move it up or down. Speculative a genuine conceptual analogy or philosophical position, not an empirical claim. Refuted made a testable commitment and lost. Untestable no instrument, unit, or observation could count against it — not a synonym for disproven; a different and worse epistemic status. Misattributed a fabricated quote, a false genealogy, or a category error.

Two binding rules. The grading-object rule: the badge grades the correspondence as popularly asserted — “occult concept X corresponds to real phenomenon Y” — not X alone and not Y alone; where a row pairs a superseded concept with a real analog, the verdict states the two statuses separately. The split-verdict precedence rule: where a claim mixes a testable component with an unfalsifiable core, the badge grades the core claim; any testable sub-component that committed and failed is recorded in the verdict as “testable component: refuted,” never silently absorbed into the badge.

Most occult–physics correspondences fail not because the physics side is absent but because of an equivocation — a real, measurable quantity is silently swapped for an undefined homonym that carries no units, no instrument, and no distinguishing prediction. The Atlas · structural finding

Part II

Historical Experiment Reconstructions

Standing Safety & Scope Disclaimer

Several reconstructions below involve lethal hazards: kilovolt-to-megavolt potentials (A4–A6), neurotoxic mercury vapor (A3, A4), antimony/arsenic fusions above 600 °C (A1, A2), molten-metal splash, and strong magnetic fields (B2). These descriptions are scholarly reconstructions for historical and analytical purposes — not DIY instructions.

Real bench work belongs in institutional laboratories with fume extraction, HV interlocks, mercury spill protocol, and IRB review where human subjects are involved. High voltage, mercury, and molten antimony kill competent people; historical fidelity is not worth a funeral.

A1 · Newton’s Star Regulus of Antimony Replicated

Basis. Cambridge UL Add. MS 3975, working from Starkey’s (“Philalethes”) recipes including the Clavis (shown by Newman 1987 to be copied from an unpublished Starkey MS). Newton made the starred regulus repeatedly; Newman and Principe replicated it for BBC/NOVA.

Modern principle. Metallothermic reduction: iron strips sulfur from stibnite (FeS enters the slag), saltpeter oxidizes excess sulfur; the star is the facet pattern of large columnar antimony crystals growing under slow, slag-capped directional solidification — a quality-control indicator readable off a phase diagram. Observable: metallic button with radiating crystalline surface (rays 5–20 mm), density ~6.7 g/cm³, XRD pure rhombohedral Sb.

Hazards: antimony fumes toxic; arsenic a common stibnite contaminant; saltpeter–sulfide mixtures are thermite-adjacent.

A2 · The Net of Vulcan Replicated

Basis. Add. MS 3975 f.43r; equated at f.54v with Philalethes’ “oak.” Ovid’s myth read as a recipe: fire + iron (via the martial regulus) + copper.

Modern principle. Cu–Sb(–Fe) ternary solidification: purple Cu₂Sb/Cu₃Sb intermetallics; the reticulated surface is a shrinkage-and-eutectic texture — last liquid outlining the primary dendrite network — and the central depression is the pipe cavity of directional solidification. Observable: purple-bronze ingot, mm-scale mesh, SEM-EDS resolving Cu₂Sb/Cu₃Sb and Fe inclusions. Newton decoded a myth into a foundry recipe whose success criterion is a microstructure.

A3 · The Sophick Mercury Attempt Documented failure

Basis. Newton’s copy (1670–78) of Starkey’s Praeparatio mercurii ad lapidem… (Science History Institute). Newton tracked the gravimetric protocol — the mercury should gain “fermental virtue,” not weight. Documented verdict: no evidence he ever succeeded; the May 1681 “doves” and caduceus insights are crossed out in the notebook — self-cancelled.

Modern principle. Amalgam chemistry and dendritic growth: supersaturated amalgams precipitate metal as fractal dendrites — diffusion-limited aggregation. The alchemists correctly observed dendritic growth but misread a transport phenomenon as animation with life. The gravimetric test was an attempted null-result mass balance, remarkable for 1670. Observable: mercury stays mercury; dendrites, if grown, are Ag/Au/Sb intermetallics by SEM-EDS. The honest reconstruction is a falsified hypothesis preserved in a notebook — scientific content of the first rank.

Hazards: mercury vapor is a cumulative neurotoxin; reconstruction belongs in a mercury-certified analytical lab only.

A4 · The Tesla Coil as Resonant Transformer Established

Basis. US 568,176 (1896), US 593,138 (1897), US 609,245 (1898): the disruptive-discharge oscillator, the λ/4 resonant secondary, the mercury-jet interrupter.

Modern principle. A spark-gap-switched, double-tuned, loosely coupled (k ≈ 0.1–0.2) resonant transformer; each discharge rings the primary tank and energy transfers at the shared resonance. The secondary is a helical resonator with self-capacitance; voltage magnification follows from Q: Vout ≈ Vin·√(L₂/L₁) — “thousands of times” in the low-loss limit. Observable: streamer length scaling ~√(input power); clean quarter-wave voltage profile along the column by field probe.

Hazards: lethal primary voltages, RF burns, ozone/NOx, EMI to implants. Do not build the mercury interrupter; a rotary gap or solid-state driver is the safe analog. Observe museum demonstration coils unless HV-qualified.

A5 · Colorado Springs & the July 3, 1899 Standing-Wave Observation Observation real Attribution wrong

Basis. Colorado Springs Notes: 110-ft lab, 50 kV supply, 51-ft secondary, 142-ft mast, 3.5–4 MV, >100-ft streamers; the ground-connected coherer receiver waxing and waning in ~30-minute intervals as a storm receded; measured wavelengths 25–70 km.

Modern principle. The receiver recorded amplitude beats of natural lightning radio energy: ground-wave/ionosphere-reflected-wave interference — classic skywave/groundwave fading — not terrestrial eigenmodes. The observation was real; the attribution was wrong. Observable today: periodic fading correlated with storm distance, not with terrestrial eigenfrequency — reproducible with a loop antenna, an SDR, and lightning data, which is the reconstruction we recommend.

Hazards: multi-MV, building-size machines are categorically non-reconstructible outside national-lab facilities. The receiving experiment is safe and cheap.

A6 · Tesla’s Earth-Resonance Scheme (US 787,412) Intuition vindicated Scheme refuted

Basis. The 1905 patent (filed 1900) and US 1,119,732 (Wardenclyffe). Requirements: polar diameter an odd multiple of the quarter wavelength; frequency below ~20 kHz (“the lowest… six per second”); wave train ≥ 0.08484 s — verbatim, the time “passing to and returning from the region diametrically opposite the pole over the earth’s surface” at Tesla’s assumed 471,240 km/s: one full ~40,000 km surface transit (40,000 ÷ 471,240 ≈ 0.0849 s), where 471,240 km/s = (π/2)·c — a surface phase velocity, not a signal speed, a point the examiner raised in 1903.

Honest modern verdict. The intuition — Earth has EM eigenmodes in the single-digit Hz range — was vindicated fifty years later in the ionosphere-cavity sense (Schumann 1952; Balser & Wagner 1960). The engineering scheme — “tens of thousands of horsepower” at <5% loss over 12,000 miles — is not supported: cavity Q ≈ 4–6, negligible coupling of a small transmitter to global modes, mode energy ~1 pT, no concentrating mechanism. Context: Trump’s 1943 review judged the late work “speculative, philosophical, and somewhat promotional… no new sound, workable principles or methods.”

Observable. With a real induction-coil magnetometer: Schumann peaks at 7.83/14.1/20.3 Hz drifting ±0.5 Hz diurnally, tracking global tropical lightning chimneys — and no response to any local ELF oscillator at planetary scale.

A7 · Bentov/Gateway ~7 Hz Body Resonance — and the Real 0.1 Hz Analog Coupling chain absent

Basis. Bentov’s Stalking the Wild Pendulum (1977), adopted as the physics model of the CIA Gateway report (1983): heart/aorta ~7 Hz standing wave resonating with Earth’s ~7.5 Hz field.

Modern principle. Two channels, kept strictly separate. (1) Mechanical: the ~7 Hz body resonance is genuine biomechanics — passive resonance of the body-on-support system driven by cardiac recoil (ballistocardiography, 4–8 Hz). (2) Electromagnetic: Earth’s static field ~50 µT; Schumann magnetic ~1 pT; human biomagnetics ~10–50 pT cardiac, ~100 fT cortical. The Gateway claim asserts channel (1) resonates with channel (2) — a category error unless a coupling chain (mechanical oscillation → EM transduction → field at distance) is specified, and none exists or has been proposed at any relevant amplitude. A mechanical resonance and a magnetic amplitude are incommensurable quantities. The real, testable autonomic resonance is at 0.1 Hz: resonance-frequency breathing maximizing HRV via the baroreflex — the quantity HeartMath rebrands as “coherence.”

Observable. Seat-accelerometer peak at 4–8 Hz tied to heartbeat; HRV maximum at individually calibrated breathing frequency (~0.08–0.11 Hz); zero measurable coupling of body oscillation to ambient ELF beyond instrument noise.

A8 · The Kozyrev Mirror as Sensory-Deprivation Device Time energy untestable

Basis. Spiral aluminum chambers, Novosibirsk, 1990s (Kaznacheev & Trofimov), invoking Kozyrev’s “time energy”; claimed ESP/visions with “95% telepathy success.”

Modern principle. A purpose-built altered-state induction chamber: acoustic/visual attenuation, partial RF shielding (a partial Faraday enclosure), and ritual expectancy — the family of effects Feynman explored in isolation tanks and found subjective but unremarkable. There is no “time energy” observable: no instrument, no unit, no prediction. Observable: elevated reports of hallucination, timelessness, presence; EEG shift toward alpha/theta; no anomalous information transfer under blinding (compare Stargate: disputed statistics, no operational utility, terminated 1995).

Caution: depersonalization and anxiety reactions in susceptible subjects; screening and informed consent required (IRB territory).

A9 · Chladni/Faraday Cymatics Plate — the Physics/Metaphysics Boundary Established

Basis. Chladni (1787): sand collects at nodal lines of vibrating plates. Faraday (1831): parametric subharmonic surface waves. Modern extension: the CymaScope — a genuine instrument with legitimate dolphin-echolocation work, wrapped in unsupported extensions (“sound heals via piezoelectricity”; “432 Hz cymaglyphs prove sacred geometry”).

Modern principle. The patterns are eigenmodes of the plate’s wave equation under its boundary conditions (∇⁴w = (ρh/D)ω²w). Change the boundary and the “sacred geometry” changes; the geometry lives in the boundary conditions, not the cosmos. Faraday waves add parametric resonance past a Mathieu-instability threshold. Safe, cheap, fully reconstructible — the rigged test of the boundary is B6.

A10 · Wever’s Bunker ~10 Hz Circadian Experiments Real Unreplicated at scale

Basis. Rütger Wever, Max Planck Institute, Erling-Andechs bunker, 1960s–70s: subjects lived weeks underground, electromagnetically shielded; circadian rhythms free-ran (~25+ h, with internal desynchronization); a weak artificial ~10 Hz field partially re-entrained some subjects. Lore to reject: “astronauts sicken without Schumann; spacecraft carry Schumann simulators” — wellness folklore without documentary basis.

Modern principle. A test of whether weak ELF fields act as a zeitgeber for the circadian oscillator — König’s hypothesis. Honest status: real and careful but small-N, never independently replicated at scale; this is the gap B1 is designed to close. Observable: free-run period >24 h shielded; partial re-entrainment under the field, judged against preregistered actimetry effect-size thresholds.

Caution: prolonged isolation carries psychological risks; fields are µT-class, far below ICNIRP limits. IRB, screening, stop-button mandatory.

Original Hypotheses with Test Protocols

Energy-scale clause · binding on all of Part B
No field–biology or quantum–consciousness hypothesis is publishable on this site unless it states its coherence timescale, its substrate, its falsifier, and its coupling amplitude against the relevant noise floor (thermal, geomagnetic, instrumental). Every entry displays its energy-scale check; the trivially satisfied cases are labeled rather than silently exempted. The preregistration commitment — including null-result publication, promised in B1 — is binding on all of Part B, in writing, on this page. Tiers: T1 <$5k garage-scale; T2 $5k–100k university lab; T3 $100k–1M facility-scale; T4 >$1M or multi-institution.

B1 · Schumann–EEG Coherence, Done Right Tier T3

Claim. Human EEG shows significant coherence/phase-locking with the ambient Schumann magnetic field beyond matched-surrogate predictions — a replication-grade test of Pobachenko 2006, Saroka 2016, Cherry 2002.

Energy-scale check (stated as the design’s premise). The Schumann magnetic component is ~1 pT; its electric component ~300 µV/m is ~4 orders of magnitude below endogenous cortical fields (mV/mm class), and its magnetic component below any known transduction threshold (an external 1 pT actually exceeds the ~100 fT cortical scalp field — the deficit is in transduction, not raw amplitude). Wang 2019 used ~50 µT — seven orders stronger — and is unreplicated. No candidate transducer (radical-pair, magnetite torque, ephaptic) operates at picotesla ELF against kT-dominated noise. B1 is therefore designed and funded as a bound-setting null measurement: the deliverable is the tightest shielded-room upper bound on SR–EEG coupling yet published. The ~5% positive prior is an explicitly labeled subjective allowance for unknown-unknown transduction, not a mechanism-based estimate.

Protocol (preregistered). N ≥ 60, 4 × 2-hour sessions (shielded/unshielded counterbalanced, subject blind); two-axis induction-coil magnetometer (noise ≤10 fT/√Hz) colocated with 64-channel EEG on a shared GPS-disciplined clock; remote reference magnetometer ≥50 km away to separate global from local ELF. Endpoints: magnitude-squared coherence at 7.83 ± 0.5 and 14.1 Hz; phase-locking in 200–300 ms windows; Schumann-envelope-to-alpha coupling — all against phase-randomized surrogates and ±1-day time-shifted pairing, cluster-corrected. Success: coherence above the surrogate 99.9th percentile in ≥5 contiguous channels, across ≥3 sessions, abolished under shielding while endogenous alpha remains. Shielding caveat: µ-metal attenuation degrades steeply below ~10 Hz — it must be measured at 7.83 and 14.1 Hz, with an active-cancellation Helmholtz fallback, and the measured shielding factor analyzed as a continuous regressor rather than a binary condition.

Falsification. Null if coherence matches surrogates in >95% of subjects, survives shielding, or tracks the local rather than the remote magnetometer. Hazard: publication bias only — preregistration and null-result publication committed.

B2 · Whole-Body Magnetic Atlas by OPM Array Tier T3–T4

Claim. A dense optically pumped magnetometer array can produce the first whole-body, room-temperature human magnetic atlas — cardiac 10–50 pT, cortical ~100 fT, muscular, gut — and quantify that inter-person magnetic coupling sits ~10⁶–10⁹ below any physiologically relevant threshold. This converts “what is a person’s frequency/aura?” into an instrumented measurement with units, replacing the debunked Tainio scale and biophoton-aura category error with data.

Energy-scale check. Cardiac source ~10–50 pT decaying ~r⁻³ to ~fT at 1 m partner distance, against a 1–5 fT/√Hz sensor floor and a ~50 µT static background — the two-person coupling measurement is a noise-floor race, and the predicted sub-threshold outcome is itself the bound.

Protocol. 64–128 SERF-regime OPMs on a conformal gantry in a shielded room; 30 volunteers at rest and under tasks (breath-hold, meditation, paced 0.1 Hz breathing); spectral atlas 0.05–200 Hz per region; dyad coupling measurements at 0.5/1/2 m. Falsification: the “biofield interaction” claim fails if partner-distance cardiac fields fall below 10 fT as dipole/quadrupole decay predicts. Confounds: OPM motion artifacts, dental metal, clothing, residual gradients.

B3 · “The Frequency of an Object” as Science Tier T2

Claim. Any object possesses a rigorous multi-modal resonance fingerprint — mechanical eigenfrequencies, spin resonances (NMR/ESR), molecular vibrations (THz/Raman) — and these, not a scalar “MHz value,” are what science can mean by an object’s frequency.

Energy-scale check. Not a field–biology claim; all signals are macroscopic against instrumental noise floors orders of magnitude lower — the clause is satisfied trivially, and we say so.

Protocol. Impulse hammer + laser-Doppler vibrometer (20 Hz–100 kHz); benchtop NMR (¹H at 42.6 MHz/T) and X-band ESR (~9.5 GHz); THz time-domain spectrometer. Test set: single-crystal quartz, brass bell, blown glass, hardwood, a singing bowl. Preregistered feature classification. Predicted: pure materials show linewidths ≪1% of center frequency; composites show hundreds of overlapping modes and no single frequency. Falsification of the fringe claim: no specimen yields a scalar frequency that survives changing boundary conditions — mount the bowl differently and its “frequency” changes, proving eigenmodes belong to the object-plus-boundary system (cf. A9).

B4 · HRV Group Synchrony Test Tier T1–T2

Claim. Dyads/groups in 0.1 Hz HRV “coherence” show interpersonal physiological synchrony beyond sham-distance and sham-time controls — the HeartMath Global Coherence claim, rigorously tested, with the Global Consciousness Project’s failure mode as the cautionary precedent.

Energy-scale check. The field channel (partner cardiac field at 0.5–2 m, ~fT after r⁻³ decay) sits at or below the OPM noise floor and 10⁷–10¹⁰ below the ~50 µT background; the behavioral channel (audible/visible pacing cues) is suprathreshold by ~60 dB. Any synchrony found is overwhelmingly more plausibly behavioral than electromagnetic — and the OPM arm quantifies that statement rather than asserting it.

Protocol (preregistered). N ≥ 100 dyads; conditions (a) face-to-face co-meditation at paced 0.1 Hz, (b) behind a 5 m opaque partition, (c) unpaced, (d) sham pairing with a time-shifted recording of a stranger. Recording length: ≥20 minutes (≥120 cycles at 0.1 Hz) per condition — five-minute recordings yield ~30 cycles and unstable Hilbert phase, and are excluded. Endpoint: cross-correlation of instantaneous HRV phase (0.08–0.12 Hz). Decisive test: (a) vs. (d) — if live-partner synchrony does not exceed time-shifted-stranger synchrony above the surrogate 99th percentile, the field claim is falsified and the residue is behavioral entrainment. Predicted: behavioral synchrony (a > b > c) at r ≈ 0.2–0.4.

B5 · Binaural-Beat Dose–Response (Gateway F10 Analog) Tier T1–T2

Claim. Binaural beats produce frequency-following EEG phase-locking with measurable dose–response, and the Gateway 4 Hz / “Focus 10” protocol shows no superiority over matched controls.

Energy-scale check. Stimulus ~60 dB SPL versus auditory threshold; expected beat-frequency steady-state response ~nV–µV against spontaneous EEG of 10–100 µV — signal-to-noise is the entire experimental problem, addressed by inter-trial phase coherence statistics, not assumed away.

Protocol (preregistered). N ≥ 80 within-subject crossover; carriers 100–500 Hz with beat differences 2/4/7/10/16/40 Hz plus monaural-beat and sham controls, double-blinded by automated randomization; 5-minute exposures. Endpoints: FFT and ITPC at the beat frequency vs. ±2 Hz flanks; dose–response fit; subjective state scales; the F10-analog test (4 Hz + guidance vs. sham beat + identical guidance). Success requires ITPC above the surrogate 99th percentile, topographically consistent, with monotonic dose–response. Falsification of the Gateway-specific claim: sham-beat guidance yielding indistinguishable state scores and EEG (predicted for the metaphysics; a modest real effect at the beat frequency is the predicted physiology). Confounds: line noise, alpha rebound, expectancy, imperfect blinding — include a discrimination check; seizure-history screening.

B6 · The Cymatic Archetype Test Tier T1–T2

Claim. Cymatic patterns’ perceived meaning is fully explained by eigenmode structure (symmetry order, complexity), not by the driving frequency’s cosmological identity (432 vs. 440 Hz) — a frequency-label effect test in a multi-cultural convenience sample. Scope honesty: N ≥ 200 raters from ≥3 cultural backgrounds supports a claim about label effects in the sample tested; the word “cross-cultural” in the ethnographic sense would require a probability-sampled frame.

Energy-scale check. The stimulus is macroscopic and suprathreshold; the claim under test concerns label effects on ratings, not field coupling — no transduction chain is invoked.

Protocol (preregistered). Stage 1 (machine): sweep 100–2000 Hz on three plate geometries; extract morphology features (Fourier ring symmetry order, fractal dimension, Euler characteristic); show patterns cluster by mode, not nominal frequency. Stage 2 (human, double-blind): raters score randomized images for meaningfulness/beauty/sacredness; identical mode shapes presented under crossed frequency labels (432/440/528). Predicted: ratings track symmetry/complexity (β > 0.3) and not frequency label (|β| < 0.05, equivalence-tested against a preregistered SESOI). Falsification: fails if the label — physically decoupled from pattern — still predicts ratings (indicating rater numerology beliefs, measured via a belief covariate, not physics).

B7 · Alchemical Replication as Analytical Chemistry Tier T2

Claim. The Philalethan “signs” — the star, the net, the purple color — are identifiable phase-diagram and solidification phenomena, and Newton’s success/failure criteria map one-to-one onto measurable microstructure and composition: alchemy as archaeometry.

Energy-scale check. Bulk-phase chemistry against analytical detection limits (XRD ~1 wt% phase fraction; ICP-MS sub-ppb traces) — all signals orders of magnitude above instrument noise; satisfied by the analytical methods themselves.

Protocol. Replications per A1/A2 under thermocouple-logged cooling curves, varying cooling rate (0.1–10 K/min), purity, and Fe content; ≥12 melts per cooling-rate cell, ≥60 melts total — at n = 12 the faceting-index vs. cooling-rate correlation has power ≈ 0.83 (α = 0.01, Fisher z) for a true r ≥ 0.8, making the preregistered criterion statistically attainable. Predicted: the star appears iff cooling proceeds below a threshold rate through the Sb liquidus under a slag-capped gradient; the net appears in a defined Cu:Sb window (predicted 55–75 wt% Cu); purple tracks Cu₂Sb surface fraction. Falsification of the sign-reading: stars/nets appearing outside the recipe window, or failing reproducibly within it, would break the sign-logic as a proxy for process state. The sophic-mercury branch (A3) runs analytically only, mercury restricted to a certified lab; predicted: no gravimetric anomaly beyond amalgam uptake — Newton’s documented null.

Hazards (highest tier in this catalog): antimony/arsenic fume, molten metal, mercury. Institutional lab, industrial hygiene sign-off, mercury protocol; no exceptions.

B8 · Microtubule Optics Under Anesthetic Modulation Tier T3–T4

Claim. Anesthetics modulate tryptophan-network UV superradiance (Babcock 2024) and exciton diffusion length (~6.6 nm; Kalra 2023) in microtubules, tracking anesthetic potency — a constrained wet-lab test at the edge of the Orch-OR program, without claiming consciousness.

Energy-scale check. The measured quantity is UV fluorescence/superradiance (~10¹⁵ Hz) against a shot-noise-limited sub-ns detector floor; the claim-relevant mismatch is timescale — optical µs/ns events versus 10⁻³–10⁻¹ s neural integration times — and that mismatch is displayed, not bridged. Tegmark decoherence (10⁻¹³–10⁻²⁰ s) and Donadi 2021 stand regardless of outcome.

Protocol (preregistered). Blinded compound coding; ≥5 concentrations per agent; the anesthetic series extended to n ≥ 8 named agents (isoflurane, sevoflurane, desflurane, halothane, propofol, etomidate, ketamine, xenon) plus ≥4 lipophilic non-anesthetic controls; optional epothilone-B stabilization arm (cf. Khan 2024, d ≈ 1.9, single-lab — needs replication). Success threshold: potency-ranked quenching correlating with clinical MAC at Spearman ρ > 0.8 across n ≥ 8 agents, absent for inactive controls — at n = 8, ρ = 0.8 gives one-tailed p ≈ 0.017; the previous n = 4 version was underpowered by construction (a perfect correlation yields only p ≈ 0.042). If the series cannot be extended, the MAC endpoint is explicitly downgraded to descriptive and inference reverts to the fully powered agent-vs-control dose–response contrast. Falsification: flat dose–response or equal quenching by controls. Either way the result constrains — never proves — Orch OR; that boundary must be stated in any publication.
