A Better Starting Point

Resonant Vessels began as a first attempt. Its useful questions survive; its claims of complete decipherment and verified simulations need narrower evidence. This revision records the corrections, keeps alternative readings visible, and turns the next questions into models that can fail.

What changed. We corrected the Clavis attribution and its DOI; withdrew one-to-one mappings from alchemical signs to substances; replaced uncertified chemical setup plates with nonprocedural interpretation cards; and marked all six inherited simulation families as unreproduced. No human experiment, healing effect, transmutation, free-energy device or antigravity result is established by this revision.

One observation can have many causes

The old B7 proposal assumed that a visual sign maps one-to-one to material composition. A small counterexample makes the missing premise visible. Let c contain three nonnegative relative amounts, and let two normalized measurement channels be y = A c, where A = [[1,0,1],[0,1,1]]. The amounts are dimensionless and are not constrained to sum to one.

Every c(t) = (0.2,0.4,0.3) + t(−1,−1,1), for −0.3 ≤ t ≤ 0.2, gives the same observation (0.5,0.7). The direction (−1,−1,1) is in the kernel. Appending [0,0,1] yields a square matrix with determinant 1. Appending [1,0,1] repeats the first channel. The pictured candidates use t = 0 and t = 0.1. If an independent total-amount measurement is known, that extra constraint can also remove this particular ambiguity; the model must state what is already measured.

Computational acceptance contract. Verify the exact products and determinant, report rank and domain nullity = number of columns − rank, test the duplicate-channel control, then introduce declared synthetic noise. A compact SVD of a 2×3 matrix returns only two singular values; their positivity does not show that the map is injective. This example matches the frozen Round 3 research contract. The algebra is directly checkable; executed numerical results belong to a separately versioned run and are not claimed by this illustration.

  • 5,000 works. The source article’s abstract and body use different wording. The body counts citation occurrences and describes hundreds of texts. Future synthesis uses “over 5,000 bibliographic references,” not 5,000 unique books. H18.
  • Fully deciphered processes. Replaced with source-specific interpretations. Exact surface phase, purity, color mechanism, growth mechanism and unique chemical dictionaries are not inferred from illustrations.
  • Sophic mercury. Separated historical preparations from the claimed transmuting agent. An unsuccessful note is not silently upgraded to a modern universally falsifying experiment.
  • B7 one-to-one mapping. Withdrawn. Replaced with a computational identifiability contract and an independent-versus-duplicate-assay control. Removed unsupported material windows, “if and only if” claims and physical replication instructions.

  • Six verified simulations. Regraded as inherited reports. Every retained numerical instrument panel carries that status; its PASS label is now REPORTED. The former “rerun everything” promise is replaced by a recovery ledger.
  • Preregistration. Protocols are proposed where a dated registered artifact was not recovered. Proposal thresholds are not findings.
  • Success probabilities and priority. Removed subjective discovery odds from the experiments table. Narrowed “first whole-body atlas,” universal fingerprints and linewidth predictions to selected measurements and hypotheses.
  • Infrasound assurance. Withdrew “trivially safe.” A weighting conversion and general limit did not validate an exposure. Replaced that card with a measurement-chain simulation question and removed its exposure setup illustration.
  • Tesla boundary-model and unit correction. Removed “mode energy ~1 pT”: picotesla is magnetic flux density, not energy. Separated Tesla’s terrestrial-conduction proposal from later Earth–ionosphere modes, withdrew automatic vindication/refutation by frequency resemblance or cavity Q, and required a mechanism-specific propagation and source-to-load audit. The reviewed patent does not validate global efficiency.
  • Final claim-scope pass. Atlas rows 4, 6, 7, 15, 22, 32 and 38 now distinguish underdefined language, subjective reports and testable acquisition/coupling claims. Lack of a verified effect is not universal refutation. Removed the remaining B1 discovery odds, funding/priority implications and promise that a null closes the wider historical question. The roundtable’s local decisions now match these corrections; failed notebook entries are not called modern universally falsifying trials. This was a review of named passages, not an exhaustive new literature census.
  • Expert dialogue. The roundtable is labeled model-generated editorial discussion, not human peer review. Its earlier “verified” statements are historical claims rather than current validation.

Newton’s 2060: read the qualification too

Yahuda MS.7.3g contains the arithmetic 800 + 1260 = 2060 within a prophetic chronology. Newton allows a later date and immediately explains that he intends to restrain rash predictions. This is a conditional theological interpretation, not a certain physical end-of-world forecast. H05.

Jung/Pauli supply a historical dialogue about symbols and interpretation; this review accessed institutional context, not the whole correspondence. Penrose’s proposed gravity-related reduction timescale is a model proposal; its abstract does not support healing or an entire-body frequency. Feynman’s methodological contribution here is explicit alternative explanations and publication of adverse outcomes. Reading scope.

Next work, selected through feedback

  1. Source and model gate. Freeze one historical passage, its genre, a translation dictionary, units and observable. Select the sharpest competing explanation. For Tesla mechanisms, bind all sources, loads and energy storage; for Schumann questions, name the Earth–ionosphere observable and measurement chain.
  2. Computational discriminator. Execute the selected model and a damaging control. Record code revision, dependencies and results. For alchemical identification, use the explicit H-N1 mixture family above. For branching, match one summary under different generators before comparing time-resolved predictions.
  3. Review and adaptive choice. Publish accepted narrow results, failures and unresolved parent claims separately. Choose the next experiment from the failure, not from a preferred conclusion. Recover earlier simulation artifacts or label any replacement as a fresh reconstruction.

Historical symbols may inspire a model; they do not supply its missing coupling, energy budget or measurement. The source graph therefore uses separate edges for authored, copied, interprets, inspires, predicts and tested by.
